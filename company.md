# Vantage Field Services

**Industry:** HVAC / Plumbing  
**Size:** 45 employees  
**Cities:** Toronto, Etobicoke, DanForth
**Founded:** 2014  

## What They Do
Residential and commercial HVAC installation, maintenance, and emergency repair. Plumbing services including pipe work, fixtures, and drain cleaning.

## Teams
- **Field:** 30 technicians across 3 cities
- **Dispatch:** 5 staff coordinating jobs and scheduling
- **Sales:** 4 reps handling new leads and quotes
- **Admin/Finance:** 6 staff handling invoicing, payroll, and reporting

## Key Data
- ~120 jobs per week
- Average invoice: $850
- Lead-to-job conversion rate: ~35%
- Top pain points: missed follow-ups on leads, slow invoice approval, manual dispatch scheduling
